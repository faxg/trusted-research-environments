# Overview

## About Trusted Research Environments

Trusted Research Environments (TREs) are specialized computing environments designed to enable research on sensitive data while maintaining the highest standards of security and governance.

### Key Characteristics

**Security & Privacy**
: TREs implement comprehensive security controls to protect sensitive data, including:
- Data encryption at rest and in transit
- Multi-factor authentication
- Role-based access control
- Audit logging and monitoring

**Controlled Access**
: Access to TREs is carefully managed and monitored:
- Researcher vetting and authorization
- Time-limited access grants
- Secure data ingress and egress controls

**Compliance**
: TREs are designed to meet various regulatory and compliance requirements:
- GDPR compliance
- Healthcare data protection (HIPAA, etc.)
- Research ethics standards

## Use Cases

TREs are commonly used for:

- Healthcare and biomedical research with patient data
- Social science research with sensitive personal information
- Financial data analysis
- Government and policy research
- Cross-institutional collaborative research

## Technology Stack

Modern TREs typically leverage:

- Cloud infrastructure (Azure, AWS, GCP)
- Containerization and orchestration
- Identity and access management systems
- Data classification and cataloging tools
- Secure workspace solutions

## This Project

This documentation site aims to be a central hub for TRE knowledge, bringing together resources from across the community to support practitioners in building, operating, and using trusted research environments.
